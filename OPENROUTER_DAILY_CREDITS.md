# OpenRouter Credits & Daily Limits Guide

## 🎁 Understanding OpenRouter Credits System

### ⚠️ Important: No Daily Credits Reset
**OpenRouter does NOT provide daily free credits that reset.** Instead, they use a **credit purchase system** combined with **daily request limits**.

### How It Actually Works:

#### 1. **Free Tier (New Users)**
- **Free Models Only:** Limited to free/open-source models
- **Daily Limit:** 50 requests per day
- **Rate Limit:** 20 requests per minute
- **Credits Required:** No credits needed for free models
- **Reset:** Daily limit resets at midnight UTC

#### 2. **After Purchasing Credits ($10+)**
- **Daily Limit Increases:** Up to 1,000 requests per day for free models
- **Paid Models:** Can use paid models (requires credits)
- **Credits:** Must purchase credits to use paid models
- **Reset:** Daily request limit resets at midnight UTC

#### 3. **Credits System**
- **Purchase Credits:** Buy credits to use paid models
- **No Daily Reset:** Credits don't reset daily - you buy them
- **Expiration:** Credits may expire after 1 year of purchase
- **Usage:** Credits are consumed per API request (varies by model)

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: Do I get free credits every day?
**A:** No. OpenRouter doesn't provide daily free credits. You get:
- **Free Tier:** 50 requests/day for free models (no credits needed)
- **After $10 Purchase:** 1,000 requests/day for free models
- **Paid Models:** Require purchased credits (no daily reset)

### Q: When do my request limits reset?
**A:** Daily request limits reset at **midnight UTC** each day. Your purchased credits do NOT reset.

### Q: How do I get more credits?
**A:** You must **purchase credits** - they don't reset daily. Go to https://openrouter.ai/settings/credits and click "Add Credits".

### Q: Can I use free models without credits?
**A:** Yes! Free models don't require credits, but you're limited to 50 requests/day on free tier.

### Q: What happens if I buy $10 in credits?
**A:** Your daily limit for free models increases to 1,000 requests/day, and you can use paid models.

### Q: Do my purchased credits expire?
**A:** Credits may expire after 1 year of purchase. Check OpenRouter's terms.

### Q: Why do I have a negative balance?
**A:** You've used more credits than you have. You need to add credits to continue using paid models.
